## 🛒 Ingredients
- **½** medium onion, diced
- **1** jalapeno, diced
- **3 cloves** garlic, minced
- **1 large pinch** salt
- **1 bunch** kale (or collards or other greens), chopped
- **1 tablespoon** water
- **1 teaspoon** ground cumin
- **½ teaspoon** ground coriander
- **2 teaspoons** smoked paprika

## 👩‍🍳 Directions
1. **Fry onions and jalapeno** for 3-5 minutes, then add **garlic** and **salt** and fry for another min.
2. **Add and mix greens and water**, then fry for a few minutes.
3. **Add spices**, mix well, then fry for another few minutes until greens have wilted and darkened, but before they go soft or too dark.
