## 🛒 Ingredients
- **2 cans** curry paste
- **1** onion, sliced
- **8** makrut lime leaves
- **1 can** coconut milk
- **2 cups** water
- **6** dried dates
- **½ cup** cashews
- **800 g** firm tofu, cubed

## 👩‍🍳 Directions
1. **Fry aromatics** in large pot until translucent.
2. **Add curry paste** and cook for 3 minutes.
3. Combine **cashews, dates, coconut milk, 4 makrut lime leaves, and water** to blender and **blend for 1 minute**.
4. **Add blended ingredients to pot** with remaining **makrut lime leaves and tofu**.
5. **Simmer for 10 minutes** before serving.

## 📝 Notes
- Serve with rice and steamed vegetables
- Can add additional aromatics such as capsicum or mushrooms.
- For satay variation, substitute 1 can curry paste for 2 tablespoons curry powder and 1/2 cup peanut butter.
